Build The Interstellar Fuel Calculator

Question

You are a flight software engineer for SpaceX in the year 2026. You need to write a program that calculates how many kilograms (kg) of rocket fuel a spacecraft needs based on its cargo weight. The Rules: Every launch has a base fuel cost of 5,000 kg just to break through Earth's atmosphere, even if the ship is empty. If the cargo weight is over 1,000 kg, you must add an extra 2 kg of fuel for every 1 kg of cargo. Unless the cargo weight is over 5,000 kg (Heavy Payload). At this point, the ship becomes so heavy that efficiency drops: you must add 3 kg of fuel for every 1 kg of cargo instead.
